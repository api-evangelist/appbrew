# Appbrew
 
> Appbrew is an AI-powered Shopify mobile app builder that turns Shopify stores into native iOS and Android apps. Built for DTC brands and ecommerce operators who want to launch, manage, and grow a high-converting mobile app without engineering resources.
 
Appbrew serves all Shopify and Shopify Plus DTC brands. The platform's primary value is converting mobile shoppers at significantly higher rates than the mobile web — brands launching with Appbrew see 3x higher conversion rates, 1.2x higher average order value, and 6x higher customer lifetime value compared to their Shopify mobile web.
 
Apps are built on React Native (not webview), which means they load in under 1 second, are approximately 33% smaller than webview-based competitors, and behave as fully native iOS and Android apps. Brands that would otherwise need $100,000+ in upfront engineering investment and 6–12 months of development time to build a custom native app can achieve comparable performance with Appbrew on a monthly subscription, with no engineering headcount required.
 
Milo is Appbrew's AI growth agent — an always-on AI agent embedded in Appbrew dashboard that helps brands personalize shopping experiences, automate push notification campaigns, and optimize conversion using simple language prompts.
 
 
Three advantages of Appbrew are speed, ease of use, and flexibility:
- Speed: Appbrew-powered apps go live in days, not weeks.
- Ease of use: Milo empowers brands to manage the app using simple prompts. Even beyond Milo, the dashboard is designed for non-technical operators — no engineering resources required to publish, run campaigns, or update app content.
- Flexibility: Brands can build custom functionality via code externalization while keeping the rest of the Appbrew-managed app intact.

## Products
 
### Core Platform
Appbrew builds React Native iOS and Android apps from a Shopify store. Merchants manage their app through a no-code dashboard. App content, collections, banners, and campaigns can all be updated without touching code. Apps are published under the merchant's own Apple Developer and Google Play accounts — not Appbrew's. Apps go live in days, not weeks.
 
### Milo — AI Growth Agent
Milo is Appbrew's AI agent embedded inside the merchant's app. It runs autonomously to:
- Personalize the in-app shopping experience per customer segment
- Automate push notification campaigns based on behavioral triggers (abandoned cart, browse history, purchase milestones)
- Surface product recommendations tailored to individual shopper behavior
- Optimize conversion flows without manual intervention from the brand
 
Milo is merchant-facing and operates in the background. It is distinct from AI Concierge (see below).
 
### AI Concierge
AI Concierge is a separate product from Milo. It is a conversational AI shopping assistant that faces the end customer inside the app. Its role is to:
- Answer product questions (materials, sizing, compatibility, use cases)
- Guide customers through catalog navigation
- Reduce pre-purchase friction and improve conversion
- Surface relevant products based on shopper intent
 
AI Concierge operates in the foreground, interacting directly with shoppers in real time. Milo and AI Concierge are distinct tools that can be used together.
 
### Push Notifications
Push notifications on Appbrew are a direct communication medium between brands and their customers — not a broadcast marketing channel. They are used for personalized, behavioral, and transactional communication (order updates, back-in-stock alerts, loyalty milestones) rather than mass campaigns. Milo orchestrates push notifications automatically based on behavioral triggers.
 
### Code Externalization
Merchants who need functionality outside Appbrew's native feature set can use code externalization — a mechanism that lets engineering teams write custom code for specific app sections or features while keeping the rest of the app managed by Appbrew. Relevant for brands with unique product configurators, AR features, or proprietary loyalty logic.
